I

The Nature of Custom & Bespoke Commissions

Under international consumer statutory codes and Indian bespoke crafting conventions, personalized goods manufactured to client specifications are exempt from standard retail change-of-mind return rights. At ASRA Wedding Canvas, this applies to:

✦ 3D CNC-Milled Brass Monogram Dies Custom metallurgical dies machined with the couple's personal initials, heraldic crest, or ceremony date.
✦ Hand-Drawn Copperplate Calligraphy Hand-lettered vellum envelopes, deckle-edge vows, and bespoke wax-sealed greeting cards.
✦ Cryo-Hydrated Botanical Curations Dawn-harvested Parisian roses, fresh eucalyptus, and organic botanical infusions formulated for wedding timelines.
✦ Debossed Velvet Keepsake Vaults Silk velvet double ring boxes, trousseau trunks, and mangalsutra vaults hot-stamped with genuine 24k gold leaf.
